Work

Projects from Media Design 12 this year. Photography, photo editing in Affinity, colour and a comic.

01

Painting to Photo

Photography, group project

We recreated The Death of Chatterton, a painting by Henry Wallis from 1856. Akmaral was the actor, Polina did the lighting and staging, and I took the photo.

We picked this painting because it has one person and one light source, so we could actually set it up in a classroom.

02

Photo Series

Photography, summer 2026

Two photo series, all taken by me in summer 2026.

Coming Back is about going back to places and people: Almaty two times, a friend I hadn't seen for a long time, and my old school in Burabay.

Cats and Sunsets is about slow summer days in Turkey with my family. Every day there was the sea, the sunset and the cats.

03

Ladybug Electric

Illustration, colour theory

The same ladybug in three colour schemes: double complementary, split complementary and complementary.

I also made three bonus ladybugs with colours that have no relationship on the colour wheel, to compare them with the ones that follow a scheme.

04

Night Shift

Comic page, Affinity

A one page comic about an owl that starts work when the city goes home. "The city clocks out, and I clock in."

I made the panels, speech bubbles and sound effects like FWUMP and SKREEE in Affinity, using photos from Wikimedia Commons.

05

The Stonor Warden

Chimera, photo editing in Affinity

A chimera made from three animals: the body of a lioness, the head of a crocodile and the wings of an eagle-owl, standing in a foggy forest.

I also wrote a short field journal entry about it, as if I met it in Stonor Forest at 5:40 in the morning.

06

Urban Zoo

Photo editing in Affinity

An owl's head on a man in a checked suit, sitting in front of a diner at night.

I put it together from several photos in Affinity.
