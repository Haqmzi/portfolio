Cats and Sunsets

A poster I made for this project from two of my own photos.

About the poster

The two photos are from my photo series Cats and Sunsets, which I took in Turkey this summer: the white cat with black spots that look like eyebrows, and the last sunset of the series.

The title is the biggest thing on the poster, so you read it first. I cropped the sunset into a circle because it looks like the sun, and it also matches the round cat head in my logo. The orange wall behind the cat and the orange sky are close to the orange in the title, so the poster feels warm.

I used only my brand colours and fonts, the same as in my logo and on this site:

Ink #16130F Orange #F05A28 Cream #F4EEE4

Fonts: Anton for titles, Space Grotesk for text.
Size: A3 (297 x 420 mm).
